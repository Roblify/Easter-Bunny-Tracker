Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 3, 2026
This Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the Easter Bunny Tracker website (the "Service").

1) Overview

The Service is designed to be used without creating an account or submitting personal profile information. We aim to minimize data collection while still providing an interactive tracking experience.

2) Acceptance of This Privacy Policy

By accessing or using the Easter Bunny Tracker website, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to abide by this Privacy Policy. Continued use of the Service constitutes acceptance of this Policy as it applies at the time of use.

3) Information Processed Automatically

Like most websites, when you visit the Service your browser may share standard technical information as part of loading the page, such as your IP address, device/browser type, and the time of your request.

4) Third-Party Content (Maps, Libraries, and Delivery Networks)

The tracker uses third-party services to display map imagery/terrain and load required libraries. Specifically:

  • Mapbox — used to render the interactive globe and map imagery. Map resources may be requested from Mapbox's servers, during which standard request data (such as IP address and user agent) may be received by Mapbox.
  • Wikipedia — the city information panel may display a link to the relevant Wikipedia article for the city the Easter Bunny is currently visiting. These are standard hyperlinks; no data is sent to Wikipedia unless you choose to click the link, at which point your browser navigates to Wikipedia's website under their own privacy policy.
  • Ko-fi — the Donate section of the Help menu embeds a widget from Ko-fi. When this widget loads, your browser may send standard request data (such as IP address and user agent) to Ko-fi's servers. No payment or personal information is shared with us through Ko-fi. Any transactions you choose to make are handled entirely by Ko-fi under their own privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare — the Service uses Cloudflare as a content delivery network and security provider. All traffic to the site passes through Cloudflare's network, during which Cloudflare may process standard request data (such as IP address, browser type, and request headers) for the purposes of delivering content, providing DDoS protection, and maintaining site performance. For more information, see Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Other content delivery networks (CDNs) — required libraries and assets may be loaded from additional public CDNs, during which those providers may receive standard request data as part of delivering the content.

5) IP Address Fetching (Arrival Estimate)

The tracker uses a third-party service (ipinfo.io) to estimate your approximate location based on your IP address. This is used solely to calculate which stop on the route is closest to you and display an estimated arrival time for the Easter Bunny in your area.

Your IP address is sent to ipinfo.io for this purpose. It is not displayed publicly, shared with other users of the Service, or used for any purpose beyond generating your arrival estimate.

This estimate is approximate and may not always be accurate.

6) Local Storage

The Service uses your browser's localStorage to save your preferences between visits. This includes settings such as map style (satellite or standard), camera mode, cinematic camera angle and zoom, music preference, speed unit, streamer mode, and city panel state. This data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to any server.

7) Cookies and Analytics

The Service does not intentionally use advertising cookies.

The Service uses Umami Analytics, a privacy-focused, cookie-free analytics platform, to understand how visitors interact with the site. Umami does not use cookies, does not collect personally identifiable information, and complies with GDPR, CCPA, and PECR. The following anonymized data may be collected:

  • Page views and visit duration
  • Referrer information (e.g., the website or search engine that directed you here)
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Approximate geographic location (country-level)

This data is processed by Umami's cloud service (umami.is) and is used solely to understand overall site usage trends. No personal data is stored, and individual visitors cannot be identified. For more information, see Umami's Privacy Policy.

8) Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share your IP address with other users of the Service. Some information may be processed by third-party providers as described above (Mapbox, ipinfo.io, Umami Analytics, CDNs) to deliver maps, scripts, analytics, or optional features.

9) Data Retention

We aim to minimize data retention. Some technical logs may exist at the hosting platform or third-party provider level as part of normal operations. Any location-based processing is intended to be limited to providing the arrival estimate feature and is not retained by us.

10) Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated.

11) Contact

For privacy questions, use the Contact section in the Help menu on the tracker page.
